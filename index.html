Blood Bank - Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Blood Bank application handles information provided by users.

The Blood Bank application is designed to help manage blood donors, blood requests, patient information, hospital information, and available blood stock.

1. Information We Collect

The application may collect and store the following information.

Donor Information

  • Donor name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Blood group
  • Age or date of birth
  • Gender
  • Address

Blood Request Information

  • Requestor name
  • Requestor phone number
  • Requestor email address
  • Required blood group
  • Hospital name and details
  • Patient information

This information is entered into the application for blood donor, blood request, patient, hospital, and blood-stock management purposes.

2. How We Use the Information

The information is used to:

  • Register and manage blood donors.
  • Manage blood requests.
  • Maintain blood-stock information.
  • Display available blood groups and stock quantities.
  • Manage hospital and patient-related blood requests.
  • Help manage blood availability and requests within the application.

3. Data Storage

The Blood Bank application uses a local SQLite database to store information required for its functionality.

The SQLite database is stored locally on the user's device. The application does not upload or transmit the stored information to a remote server or cloud service.

The application does not require an Internet connection for storing or managing donor, blood request, patient, hospital, or blood-stock information.

4. Location Information

The application does not collect or access GPS or precise location information.

5. Camera and Device Permissions

The application does not use the device camera for collecting user information.

The application does not require GPS or location access for its core functionality.

6. Advertising

The Blood Bank application does not display advertisements and does not collect information for advertising purposes.

7. User Accounts

The application does not require users to create an account or log in to use its functionality.

8. Data Sharing

The application does not intentionally transmit the collected information to third-party advertising, analytics, or cloud services.

Information entered into the application is used for the application's blood donor, blood request, patient, hospital, and blood-stock management functions.

9. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored by the application. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should protect access to the device on which the application and its database are stored.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information remains in the application's local database until it is deleted through the application's available data-management functionality or the application's data is removed from the device.

If you need assistance regarding deletion of information, please contact the application developer using the contact information provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be made available at the privacy-policy URL associated with the application.
